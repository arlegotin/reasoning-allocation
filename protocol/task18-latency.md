# Task 18 prospective latency sensitivity diagnostic

This is a finite exploratory public-validation experiment, authorized by the reviewed Task17 negative A–B2 result and measured administration burden. It cannot revise that frozen result, estimate how much of A's loss was caused by administration, or support a revised candidate claim. The treatment changes elapsed time and can change the native trajectory. Constant sleep is a wall-latency stress, not a model of CPU/cache/memory effects or information quality.

Use the existing Task13 validation manifest, original generator indices 0,2,4,6,8,10: one original in each of the six cells, selected by index without outcome screening. The six originals are the units; two temporal observations of each dose are not twelve independent originals. Retain the original namespace, source hashes and generator config. No protected template, fit, new domain, resampling or primary inference is executed.

The control is the existing B2 estimate selector, length 1, pool size 128, solver/pool seed 17. Add 0, 4, or 10 ms after each cheap allocation computation, inside the charged selector administration interval. Each dose uses the same instrumentation. No new lease starts if the delay crosses the request deadline; native unwinding receives a legal live node. Parent receipt time still controls progress credit, including late final output. All cold startup, input loading, observation, finalization, release, publication and overshoot remain charged. Actual sleep intervals are nested diagnostics and must not be added again to exclusive administration.

Dose support comes from prior **public Task13 historical local asset**, not direct primary A decision latency. At the middle deadline, the historical local asset median rebid administration was 3.9090205 ms versus B2 0.354 ms; at the long deadline their means were 10.3341452 and 0.2865529 ms. Round the approximate gaps to 4 and 10 ms; no fine tuning. At the short deadline B2 made only eight rebids across six originals; at the middle it made 118, and at the long it completed all six originals. Select only the existing middle deadline 2.106828958 s for meaningful decision exposure with less ceiling saturation. These counts choose budget support, not cases. Individual instance outcomes never determine inclusion.

Run 36 cold requests serially: six originals × three doses × two timing repeats. The two identical-seed repeats are named r0 and r1; all six dose/repeat methods are shuffled within each original with existing deterministic blocked scheduling and run-order seed 2026101418. Exact order, source/environment/build/config/control/candidate/input hashes and request values are committed in task18-latency.json before timing. There is no adaptive reselection. Forecast 35–50 seconds based on the existing six-request B2 middle block costing 5.717441333 seconds; scheduled deadline sum is 75.845842488 seconds. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed upper bound: cold preparation, publication and grace add costs. One solver worker and one native thread; no heavy concurrent work.

Stop after any completed attempt with an ordinary fault, quarantine, missing resource release, failed timing instrumentation, overshoot greater than 20% of the deadline, or total coordinator elapsed reaching 180 seconds. A started attempt may run through its fixed 2-second watchdog grace and cleanup; the cap is checked between attempts. Preserve every attempted row, failure and cost; publish unexecuted count. Do not retry or extend because results are favorable, unfavorable or noisy.

Summarize all per-original/per-repeat dose contrasts in receipt-gated utility, raw primal/dual, processed nodes/LPs, actual delay, exclusive timing, CPU, RSS, trace bytes and overshoot. Average timing repeats within original first; report equal-cell mean and per-original range plus repeat spread as descriptive uncertainty. No bootstrap, p-value, population interval, practical-equivalence claim or broad robustness claim. A zero/positive average is still reported, and no further diagnostic or optimization pass follows automatically. Report the measurement to the controller before any expansion; revised promoted candidates require a new numbered amendment and fresh protected confirmation.

Population engineering is **unexecuted: gate unmet**. Generic administration cost does not identify a representation-specific time/memory advantage. Task3/AppendixA coupling and evidence-duplication checks remain analytical invariants, not native population performance. No population.py or empty parity test is created. Random bids, shuffled features, horizon expansion, independent fits, feature/pool/capacity grids, objective/bridge/long-tail shifts, cold/warm reuse and extra domains remain unexecuted: the selected test answers the narrower latency question first. Prior tasks' implemented controls remain evidence with their original limits; they are not relabeled as newly executed Task18 treatments.
